## ESMA-G1 Information to clients
Before non-advised services begin, firms should tell clients in plain language that the firm carries out the assessment and why accurate, up-to-date answers matter. Firms must keep records of what they provided.

## ESMA-G2 Collecting client information
Firms should gather the information needed for the product types they offer. Questions should be clear, clients should be able to answer that they do not know, and clients should not self-assess their knowledge.

## ESMA-G4 Reliability of information
Firms may rely on client information unless they know or ought to know it is manifestly out of date, inaccurate or incomplete. Firms should take reasonable steps to check consistency, such as seeking clarification where answers conflict.

## ESMA-G5 Up-to-date information
Where firms rely on previously collected information, they should set procedures defining how often it is updated, and should guard against anomalous updates that could make a product appear appropriate.

## ESMA-G9 Warnings
Warnings should be concise, prominent, clear and not misleading. Firms should take reasonable steps to ensure warnings are received and understood, and should explain the reason for the warning.

## ESMA-P17 Client proceeds after warning
If a client gives insufficient information or the product is deemed inappropriate, the firm should issue a warning. The client may still ask to proceed, and the firm decides whether to accept that request. Cooling-off periods are measures that limit circumvention rather than blocks on transactions.

## ESMA-G11 Record keeping
Firms should keep records of the information provided to clients, any warnings issued, and the corresponding client transactions. Records should cover the result of the assessment, any warning, whether the client asked to proceed, whether the firm accepted, how client information was collected, and the basis for not carrying out an assessment.

## ESMA-G12 Execution-only
The execution-only exemption applies to non-complex products. Firms must be able to identify whether a transaction was initiated by the client and must trace whether an order followed a personalised communication from the firm.

## ESMA-B19 Complex by default
Products not on the list of automatically non-complex instruments are treated as complex unless they have been assessed against, and meet all, the Article 57 criteria of the Delegated Regulation.
