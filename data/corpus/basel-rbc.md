## RBC20.1 Minimum capital ratios
Banks must meet three minimum risk-based capital ratios at all times, each measured against risk-weighted assets (RWA). Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) must be at least 4.5% of RWA. Tier 1 capital must be at least 6% of RWA. Total capital must be at least 8.0% of RWA. A 2.5% CET1 capital conservation buffer applies to all banks on top of these minimums, and a countercyclical buffer or higher loss absorbency requirements may also apply. This version of RBC20 is effective from 1 January 2023; a later version is scheduled to take effect on 1 January 2028.

## RBC20.2 Capital components
Capital components are defined in chapter CAP10, are used net of the regulatory adjustments in CAP30, and are subject to the transitional arrangements in CAP90.

## RBC20.3 Risk coverage and approaches
The framework covers RWA for credit risk, market risk and operational risk. It allows different calculation approaches, some of which need supervisory approval. Approaches used only for the output floor are not counted among a bank's nominated approaches.

## RBC20.4 Output floor
Total RWA is the higher of two amounts. The first is the sum of credit, market and operational RWA under the bank's nominated approaches. The second is 72.5% of the sum of those RWA calculated using only the standardised approaches listed in RBC20.11. This second element is the output floor, and it is subject to the transitional arrangements in RBC90. The higher figure is used to test compliance with the minimum ratios and buffers.

## RBC20.9 Market risk RWA
Market RWA is the sum of RWA for trading book and foreign exchange or commodity positions, under the standardised, internal models or simplified approaches, and RWA for credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk.

## RBC20.13 Output floor illustration
The illustrative floor calculation uses pre-floor RWA of 76 and standardised RWA of 140. Since 72.5% of 140 is 101.5, which is higher than 76, the bank uses 101.5 for compliance purposes.

## RBC30.1 Capital conservation buffer
The capital conservation buffer equals 2.5% of risk-weighted assets, held entirely in CET1 capital, above the regulatory minimum. Distribution limits apply when capital falls inside the buffer range, while normal banking operations are not restricted. CET1 is first used to meet minimum capital and TLAC requirements, and only the surplus counts toward the buffer.

## RBC30.4 Distribution constraints
A bank must recalculate its conservation ratio at each distribution date. With a CET1 ratio of 4.5% to 5.125%, 100% of earnings must be retained. Above 5.125% to 5.75%, 80% must be retained. Above 5.75% to 6.375%, 60% must be retained. Above 6.375% to 7.0%, 40% must be retained. Above 7.0%, no retention is required.

## RBC30.6 Countercyclical buffer
National authorities set a countercyclical buffer when excess credit growth builds system-wide risk. It ranges from zero to 2.5% of RWA and must be met with CET1. Internationally active banks apply a weighted average of the rates in the jurisdictions where they hold private-sector credit exposures.

## RBC30.11 Countercyclical buffer timing
Increases in the countercyclical buffer are pre-announced, with an effective date up to 12 months after the announcement. Decreases take effect immediately.
