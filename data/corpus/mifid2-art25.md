## Art25(1) Staff knowledge and competence
Investment firms must ensure, and be able to show regulators, that staff giving investment advice or information about instruments and services have the necessary knowledge and competence.

## Art25(2) Suitability for advice and portfolio management
Before recommending services or instruments, a firm giving advice or portfolio management must obtain information on the client's knowledge and experience, financial situation including capacity to bear losses, investment objectives and risk tolerance. Bundled packages must be suitable overall. Where switching is involved, the firm must analyse costs and benefits.

## Art25(3) Appropriateness for other services
For services other than advice and portfolio management, the firm must ask the client for information on knowledge and experience so it can assess whether the product or service is appropriate. If the firm considers it inappropriate, it must warn the client. If the client gives no information or insufficient information, the firm must warn the client that it cannot determine appropriateness. Warnings may be given in a standardised format.

## Art25(4) Execution-only exemption
A firm may skip the appropriateness assessment only for certain listed non-complex instruments, where the service is provided at the client's initiative, the client is clearly warned that no assessment is made and the corresponding protection does not apply, and the firm meets its conflict of interest obligations under Article 23.

## Art25(5) Client agreement records
Firms must keep a record of the agreed documents that set out each party's rights and obligations and the terms of the service.

## Art25(6) Reporting to clients
Firms must provide adequate reports on a durable medium, including periodic communications and costs. For advice, a suitability statement must be given before the transaction is made.

## Art25(8) Delegated acts
The Commission can adopt delegated acts specifying how firms apply paragraphs 2 to 6, taking into account the service, the product and whether the client is retail or professional.
