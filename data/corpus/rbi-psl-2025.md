## PSL-Para 2 Applicability
The directions apply to commercial banks, regional rural banks, small finance banks, local area banks and urban co-operative banks. NBFCs have no priority sector targets of their own under these directions; they appear only as on-lending intermediaries.

## PSL-Para 5 Priority sector categories
There are eight categories: agriculture, micro small and medium enterprises, export credit, education, housing, social infrastructure, renewable energy, and others. Weaker sections is an overlapping category. Each loan may be classified under only one of the eight categories.

## PSL-Para 7.1 Targets
Targets are a percentage of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC) or the Credit Equivalent of Off-Balance Sheet Exposures (CEOBSE), whichever is higher. Domestic banks and foreign banks with 20 or more branches have a total target of 40%. Foreign banks with fewer than 20 branches also have 40%, of which up to 32% may be export credit and at least 8% other priority sector lending. Regional rural banks have 75%. Small finance banks have 60%.

## PSL-Para 7.1-sub Sub-targets
For domestic banks and foreign banks with 20 or more branches, the sub-targets are 18% for agriculture, 7.5% for micro enterprises and 12% for weaker sections.

## PSL-Para 7.2 Urban co-operative banks
For urban co-operative banks the total target is 60%, with 7.5% for micro enterprises and 12% for weaker sections.

## PSL-Para 21 Priority Sector Lending Certificates
Banks may buy and sell Priority Sector Lending Certificates under RBI guidelines. The net nominal value of certificates issued and purchased is eligible for the relevant categories where the underlying assets are eligible.

## PSL-Para 23 On-lending through NBFCs
Bank credit to NBFCs other than MFIs for on-lending qualifies as priority sector lending for agriculture term lending up to Rs 10 lakh per borrower and for micro and small enterprises up to Rs 20 lakh per borrower. Disaggregated data and auditor certification are required. This is not available to regional rural banks, urban co-operative banks, small finance banks or local area banks.

## PSL-Para 24 On-lending through HFCs
Housing loans through National Housing Bank approved housing finance companies qualify up to Rs 20 lakh per borrower, with auditor certification.

## PSL-Para 25 On-lending cap
On-lending to NBFCs, including HFCs, and to NCDC is capped at 5% of the bank's previous-year total priority sector lending, averaged across four quarters.

## PSL-Para 26 Co-lending
Co-lending arrangements are permitted under the Commercial Banks Transfer and Distribution of Credit Risk Directions, 2025. Loans under the earlier 2018 and 2020 co-lending circulars stay eligible until repayment or maturity.

## PSL-Para 28 Monitoring and reporting
Priority sector compliance is monitored on a calendar-quarter basis. Quarterly data is due within 15 days after quarter end, and annual data within one month after the financial year end.

## PSL-Para 29 Shortfall treatment
Banks with a priority sector shortfall are allocated amounts for the Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF) and other funds with NABARD, NHB, SIDBI or MUDRA. Shortfalls are assessed quarterly and averaged over the year. Where the overall shortfall is below 5 percentage points, the deposit rate is Bank Rate minus 2 points; from 5 to below 10 points, Bank Rate minus 3 points; at 10 points or more, Bank Rate minus 4 points.
