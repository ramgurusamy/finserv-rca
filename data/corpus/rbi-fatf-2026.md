## FATF-CFA Call for action jurisdictions
The high-risk jurisdictions subject to a call for action are the Democratic People's Republic of Korea (DPRK), Iran and Myanmar. DPRK and Iran are covered by the February 2020 statement, which remains in effect, and Myanmar was added in October 2022.

## FATF-IM Increased monitoring jurisdictions
As of the 19 June 2026 FATF statement, the jurisdictions under increased monitoring are Angola, Bolivia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Cameroon, Cote d'Ivoire, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Haiti, Iraq, Kenya, Kuwait, Lao People's Democratic Republic, Lebanon, Monaco, Nepal, Papua New Guinea, South Sudan, Syria, Venezuela, Vietnam, Virgin Islands (UK) and Yemen.

## FATF-CHG Changes in June 2026
Bosnia and Herzegovina and Iraq were newly added to increased monitoring. Algeria and Namibia were removed.
