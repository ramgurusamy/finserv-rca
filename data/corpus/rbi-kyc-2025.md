## KYC-Para 5(1)(iv) Beneficial owner
For a company, the beneficial owner is a natural person holding more than 10% of shares, capital or profits, or exercising control. For a partnership firm the same 10% test applies to capital or profits. For an unincorporated association or body of individuals the threshold is more than 15% of property, capital or profits. If no natural person is identified, the senior managing official is treated as the beneficial owner.

## KYC-Para 5(2)(v) When CDD is required
Customer due diligence is required when opening an account-based relationship, for occasional transactions of Rs 50,000 or more whether single or connected, and for international money transfers.

## KYC-Para 17(2) CDD cannot be applied
No account may be opened where appropriate CDD cannot be applied, whether because the customer does not cooperate or the documents are unreliable. The bank should consider filing a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR).

## KYC-Para 17(3) No transaction without CDD
No transaction or account relationship may begin without following CDD.

## KYC-Para 17(9) Sanctions list check
Banks must have a system to ensure customers do not match names on sanctions lists.

## KYC-Para 18 Reasoned rejection
A bank must not reject an onboarding or periodic KYC application without application of mind, and the officer must record the reasons for any rejection.

## KYC-Para 19 Tipping-off
If the bank suspects money laundering or terrorist financing and believes that performing CDD would tip off the customer, it should not pursue CDD and should instead file an STR with FIU-IND.

## KYC-Para 20 Risk categorisation
Customers are classed as low, medium or high risk based on identity, social and financial status, business activity, location, products, delivery channels and transaction types. Banks may use the FATF Public Statement in their risk assessment. Risk categories must be kept confidential.

## KYC-Para 41 Review frequency
Risk categorisation must be reviewed at least every six months.

## KYC-Para 42 Periodic KYC updation
Periodic KYC updation is required at least every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers and every 10 years for low-risk customers.

## KYC-Para 50 Record retention
Transaction records must be kept for at least five years from the date of the transaction. Identification and address records must be kept for at least five years after the relationship ends. Records must be kept in enough detail to reconstruct individual transactions.

## KYC-Para 52 Reporting to FIU-IND
The bank must report to the Director of FIU-India under Rule 3 of the PML Rules, 2005, as Rule 7 requires.

## KYC-Para 54 Reporting delay and confidentiality
Each day of delay in reporting, or in correcting a misrepresented transaction, beyond the time limit in the Rules counts as a separate violation. A bank may not restrict account operations solely because an STR was filed, and filing must stay confidential.

## KYC-Para 55 Transaction monitoring
Banks must run software that raises alerts on transactions inconsistent with a customer's risk category and updated profile.

## KYC-Para 56 UNSC and UAPA lists
Banks must not hold accounts for individuals or entities on the UNSC Al-Qaida/ISIL and Taliban lists, must verify those lists and the UAPA Schedules daily, and must report matching accounts to FIU-IND and the Ministry of Home Affairs.

## KYC-Para 58 UNSCR 1718 list
Banks must verify the UNSCR 1718 list concerning the DPRK daily.

## KYC-Para 61 FATF-identified jurisdictions
Banks must use FATF statements circulated by RBI and public information to identify countries that do not or insufficiently apply the FATF Recommendations, and must apply risk-proportionate enhanced due diligence to relationships and transactions with them. Banks must give special attention to business with persons from those jurisdictions. This does not bar legitimate trade and business. Banks must examine the background and purpose of such transactions, keep written findings and documents, and provide them to RBI or other authorities on request.
