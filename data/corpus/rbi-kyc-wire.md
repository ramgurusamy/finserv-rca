## KYC-WIRE-Para 74(1)(i) Cross-border wire transfer information
Cross-border wire transfers must carry accurate, complete and meaningful originator and beneficiary information. Originator information is the name, the account number where an account is used, and the address, national identity number, customer identification number, or date and place of birth. Beneficiary information is the name and the account number where an account is used. If there is no account, a unique transaction reference number must allow the transaction to be traced.
