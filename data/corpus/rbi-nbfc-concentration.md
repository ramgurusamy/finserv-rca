## NBFC-CR-Para 4(5) Large exposure definition
For an Upper Layer NBFC, a large exposure is the total exposure value to a counterparty or connected group that equals or exceeds 10% of the eligible capital base, which is Tier 1 capital.

## NBFC-CR-Para 29(3) Intra-group exemption
Exposures to group entities that are deducted from Owned Funds to arrive at Net Owned Funds are exempt from the large exposure framework.

## NBFC-CR-Para 33 Single counterparty limit
An Upper Layer NBFC's total exposure to a single counterparty must stay at or below 20% of its eligible capital base at all times.

## NBFC-CR-Para 34 Board exception
The Board may allow up to 5% more, to a maximum of 25%, under a Board-approved policy and with written reasons for each exceptional case.

## NBFC-CR-Para 35 Connected group limit
Total exposure to a group of connected counterparties must stay at or below 25% of the eligible capital base at all times.

## NBFC-CR-Para 45 Breach handling
Breaches are permitted only under exceptional conditions beyond the NBFC's control. They must be reported immediately to RBI and promptly rectified, and the NBFC cannot take on further exposure to that counterparty or group until it is back within the limit.

## NBFC-CR-Para 48 Large exposure reporting
Reporting covers all large exposures, other exposures of 10% or more measured without credit risk transfer offsets, exempted exposures of 10% or more, and the 10 largest exposures regardless of size.
