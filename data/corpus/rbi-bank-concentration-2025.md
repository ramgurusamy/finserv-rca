## CR-AMD-Para 116 Intra-group exposure limits
The intra-group limits in paragraphs 116(1) and 116(2) now refer to the eligible capital base rather than paid-up capital and reserves. Exposure includes funded and non-funded credit and investment exposure, and excludes equity and other regulatory capital instruments.

## CR-AMD-Para 4(3) Eligible capital base
The eligible capital base is redefined as effective Tier 1 capital, with allowances for capital infused after the balance sheet date and, for Indian banks, profits accrued during the year.

## CR-AMD-Foreign-branches Derivatives with group
Indian banks' exposures to overseas branches, and a foreign bank's exposures to its head office and overseas branches, are generally outside the intra-group norms. Proprietary derivative transactions are the exception and count when calculating exposure.

## CR-AMD-Timing Effective date
Most amendments take effect on 1 April 2026, and banks may adopt them earlier. Existing breaches must be brought within limits within six months of issuance.
